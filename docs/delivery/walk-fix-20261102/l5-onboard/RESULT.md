## Needs owner

1. **M-17 money line.** Your no-captions rule makes this your call. Default I recommend: keep the line, because it is the only place a merchant learns, before serving, that a 权益核销 stop earns no cash per redemption. Compare `shots/M-17-after.jpg` (with) and `shots/M-17-after-without-line.jpg` (without). To drop it, delete the one `<text … wx:if="{{item.settleText}}">` line.
2. **M-15 reading.** I read 「店铺就在俱乐部中 需要跳转过去就好了」 as "the shop already has a page; just send the staff there". So after accepting, staff land on the shop workbench (the page with 扫码核销), the same page their 「我的 → 核销」 already opens. I did not change which home a staff member sees on a later cold start. If 「俱乐部」 meant the player 俱乐部 tab (`pages/talent/list`), say so and I will point the jump there. Default: the workbench.
3. **M-16 shared category table.** `sys_category type=1` feeds merchant 行业类型, the organiser's theme categories and chapter-recruitment categories, and player route preferences. The new merchant rows (书店, 茶馆, …) will therefore also show in the theme category and route-preference pickers. Chapter recruitment needs this: an organiser can now recruit 书店 specifically. Theme categories were curated on 11-01. Default: accept it for launch. Later, give merchant industries their own `type` or a flag and filter the theme picker.
4. **M-07 rejected merchant.** Once a merchant has taken over the placeholder, a rejected or withdrawn application leaves that row with the merchant's content, rejected and hidden. The chapter then shows no stop until the next merchant resubmits, and the next merchant gets a new row. Before this change the placeholder stayed visible. Default: accept it. A theme cannot go on sale with a chapter that has no approved merchant, so players only see the gap during recruiting.
5. **M-07 existing duplicates.** Themes already joined through the UI keep their extra placeholder row. Default: the organiser removes the placeholder in the editor. I did not write a data migration, because merging rows would orphan NPC config and printed check-in codes.
