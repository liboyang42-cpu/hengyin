## Lane m2-routes

Work on `integ/launch-test-20261103`, after `docs/delivery/walk-fix-20261102/integ/ROUND9-M1-RESULT.md` has a `STATUS:` line.

1. **My route.** Bright line with a soft glow (wide translucent polyline under a narrow one) while walking, in both `pages/roam` and theme games. Check what exists first. Drawn points respect existing battery, foreground and background rules. Reduce points so `setData` stays small.
2. **Same-session team routes.** Screen `m-team`, `m-consent`. Owner-approved limits: **only teams in the same session can see each other; each person decides before the start; the theme's author can turn it off.** A person who turned it off, or left, or finished, shows nowhere. Their past points aren't served. Each team gets one colour, an avatar pin with its progress, and a rail where tapping follows a team. Updates about every 10 to 15 seconds through polling with backoff, no sockets. `translateMarker` or `moveAlong` for smooth movement. Backend: ownership-checked reads and writes with paid-and-in-this-session checks, stored coarsely, deleted after the session (state the retention), never exposed on public projections, no phone or ids beyond what the UI needs. Test that a player from another session, a guest, an unpaid member and a person who opted out get nothing. Default for the consent switch: on, each person can turn it off (make this one config value so it can change). Fix the prototype's thin 开始 button.
3. **Route share card.** Screen `m-card2`. After a theme ends the route draws itself over the map and settles on a card: big number, theme name, time, stops, city, and a real mini-program code for the theme. Generated on canvas for 保存图片 and 分享. Don't let the route cross the number. Works for solo, team and free exploration. Move the existing end-of-theme review in rather than adding a second one. Never reward or require sharing. Avoid showing the exact start point of a person's home: fuzz the first and last 150 m of the route on the card.

## Lane m3-atmos

It ends on China. Clouds: raymarched fbm with soft self-shadowed undersides. No hard edges, no circles, no banding. Motion: eased, no linear moves, no shake, and the cut to clouds is hidden in the clouds. Provide `atmos.mp4`, a 16-frame contact sheet `sheet.jpg` from it and the render source. Look at the sheet yourself and redo it until it would hold up next to the reference. Say honestly if it can't. Hosting comes later.
