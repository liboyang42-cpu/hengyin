## needs owner

0. **Big two-line title on P1/P7 vs. the title contract (added after merging staging `07ce40be`).** Staging's qx-b3 fix made both pages carry an unconditional shared `cy-page-title` (enforced by `custom-nav-page-surface`, `title-system-owner` and `nav-page-title-spacing`). A second big two-line hero title under the card stack would be a duplicate title. **Default implemented:** the contract wins. The headline is the shared page title under the nav bar; the hero keeps the card stack, the muted fact line, the pill CTA and the P4 pager, and the brand-violet period is dropped on these two pages (it stays on the W2-16 intro). To get ref 1's big centred title back, the owner can approve the same exception `pages/publish/template-intro` already has (titled by its centred two-line claim) for these two routes.
1. **W2-16 step count.** LANES.md says "a three-step pager", but the owner-approved intro (qx-a6, R12) has four steps with approved copy. **Default implemented:** keep the four approved steps and add the ref 1 chrome. Dropping a step is a one-line change to `introSteps`.
2. **The P1 hero has no theme cover.** LANES.md asks for a stack "built from the theme cover". Neither landing read model (`ReferralLandingView.Target`, `GiftLandingView.Product/Item`) carries a cover, and the brief says to use only data the screen already has. **Default implemented:** the front card shows the inviter and the theme title, or a kind glyph, and no image. If the owner wants the cover, the backend (qx-c1/b3) should add an approved public cover URL to `target` / `items[]`. The card can then render it as the front layer.
3. **Period accent colour.** In the player domain, `--cy-accent`/`--cy-color-brand` resolve to white (2026-08-01 「玩家域去紫」), so a "coloured" period needs the violet atom. **Default implemented:** `--cy-ref-violet-500`, the same atom as the site-wide tab bar's selected state (UI-03).
4. **Secondary text action on P1.** Ref 1 has a text secondary action. **Default implemented:** 先逛逛 → home tab. It has no side effect, and it can be removed if the owner prefers.
